ABOUT & DISCLAIMER

This is a personal research project that analyzes declassified U.S. government UAP files. Release 01 covers 161 documents from 6 agencies — Department of War, FBI, NASA, State Department, AARO, and DVIDS — spanning 1944 to 2025. The files were organized into 7 analysis tracks and converted into slide decks. This page is an index — click any track below to view or download the research summary slides.

I'm not a UFO expert, researcher, or authority — just a curious person who read the files. This is a personal project analyzing publicly available government documents. Nothing here is professional analysis, insider information, or official conclusions. Treat it as one person's attempt to organize and understand what's in the public record — and take it all with a giant grain of salt.
